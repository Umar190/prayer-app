Privacy

Your precise coordinates and settings are stored locally on this device by Noor. They are sent to the timing/geocoding services only when you request location-based features.

Notifications

Browser notification timing can be throttled while a page is suspended. This release therefore labels reminders as “while Noor is open”; reliable background Adhan delivery should use a native/mobile push system in a later app-store build.
