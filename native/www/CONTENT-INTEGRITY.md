# Noor religious-content integrity policy

Noor follows these rules for Qur'an and Prophet content:

1. **Qur'an Arabic is source-fetched.** Noor does not generate or paraphrase Qur'anic Arabic.
2. **Qur'an translations are shown as the selected published translation**, not as Noor's own AI translation.
3. **Prophet Stories are human-written summaries with explicit Qur'an references.** They are not presented as verbatim revelation.
4. Noor avoids adding dialogue, dates, names, miracles, chains, or historical details unless they are supported by the cited source.
5. When a matter is based on moon sighting or community practice, Noor labels it as such rather than presenting one convention as universally binding.
6. Community posts are user-generated and are never presented as scholarly rulings. Users are told to cite sources and avoid presenting AI output or personal opinion as fatwa.

Before adding any new Qur'an text, hadith text, prophetic story, dua, or religious ruling, use a reviewed source and preserve the exact source attribution.
