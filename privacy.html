Noor is designed to keep personal settings and precise location on your device wherever practical.

Location

When you allow location access, Noor reads GPS coordinates in your browser/device to calculate prayer times and Qibla direction. Noor saves the most recent successful coordinates locally so it can reopen with the last known location.

External services

Prayer calculations are requested from AlAdhan using latitude/longitude. Reverse geocoding is requested from BigDataCloud to turn coordinates into a town/city label. Quran data and audio use public Quran data services described in the app documentation.

Notifications

Notification permission is controlled by your browser or device. The web version does not guarantee closed-app Adhan playback. The native app is designed to use device-scheduled local notifications.

Local data

Settings, bookmarks, reading position, prayer tracking and saved location are stored in browser local storage. Use “Reset local data” in Settings to remove Noor's locally stored data on this device.

Adhan audio

The default web Adhan source is a CC0 public-domain recording published on Wikimedia Commons. Noor does not claim ownership of that recording.
